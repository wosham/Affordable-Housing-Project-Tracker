Mandates & Accountabilities

Who Does What?

Expand each stakeholder group to understand their legal mandate, core responsibilities and how they interact with other programme actors.

Legal Basis

The Affordable Housing Programme is anchored in the Kenya Constitution 2010 Article 43(b) — the right to accessible and adequate housing — and operationalised through the Affordable Housing Act 2024.

Core Responsibilities

  • Formulate national housing policy and annual delivery targets
  • Allocate Treasury funding via the Housing Levy (1.5% of gross salary)
  • Establish and fund the Affordable Housing Board (AHB)
  • Award contracts through open national tender processes

Reporting Lines

Reports to the President via the Cabinet Secretary. AHB reports to the PS and CS. All county field offices report to the AHB CEO nationally.

View full hierarchy

Legal Basis

Operating under the County Governments Act 2012 and County Land Management Act. The county provides public land for housing sites under Article 62 of the Constitution.

Core Responsibilities

  • Identify and gazette public land for housing sites
  • Facilitate community engagement and baraza sessions
  • Issue local construction permits and way-leaves
  • Monitor project progress through ward administrators

Reporting Lines

The CEC Member for Lands & Housing reports to the Governor. The AHB Field Director (Moses Owuor) is a national officer embedded in the county.

Meet Field Director

Legal Basis

Contractors must hold a valid National Construction Authority (NCA) registration at category NCA 1–5 and comply with the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act 2015.

Core Responsibilities

  • Execute construction works as per approved Bills of Quantities
  • Maintain project schedules and submit monthly progress reports
  • Ensure site safety compliance per OSHA 2007
  • Achieve at least 30% local youth and women sub-contracting

Reporting Lines

Contractors report to the AHB Field Director and the AHB Project Manager in Nairobi. Payments are certified by an NCA-registered Clerk of Works.

View active contractors

Legal Basis

Governed by the Affordable Housing Act 2024, the Kenya Mortgage Refinance Company Act, and the Finance Act 2023 establishing the mandatory 1.5% Housing Levy.

Core Responsibilities

  • Collect and manage housing levy receipts (KRA via eCitizen)
  • Disburse construction payments in certified tranches
  • Provide affordable mortgage refinancing through KMRC
  • Operate Boma Yangu beneficiary registration and allocation

Reporting Lines

KMRC reports to the National Treasury. Boma Yangu is operated by the AHB under the CS. Financial auditing is conducted by the Office of the Auditor General.

Boma Yangu Portal

Legal Basis

NEMA operates under EMCA Cap 387; NCA under the NCA Act 2011; KNBS under the Statistics Act 2006; OAG under the Public Finance Management Act 2012.

Core Responsibilities

  • NEMA: Environmental Impact Assessment and compliance
  • NCA: Contractor registration, site inspection & quality audit
  • KNBS: Independent housing delivery and impact statistics
  • OAG: Annual programme expenditure audit reports

Independence

All four oversight bodies are constitutionally or legislatively independent from the programme implementers to ensure objective accountability.

Legal Basis

Community participation is protected under the Constitution Article 10 (national values) and Article 174 (devolution objects). The AHP prioritises vulnerable groups per the Affordable Housing Act 2024.

Entitlements & Roles

  • Register and apply for housing allocation via Boma Yangu
  • Participate in ward-level baraza and site verification meetings
  • Report construction grievances to the Field Office
  • Women & PWD groups: priority allocation under AHP regulations

Programme Journey

Key Stakeholder Engagement Milestones

From presidential directive to community baraza — a chronological record of how stakeholders have shaped this programme.

June 2022
National Govt

Presidential Directive — AHP Launch

H.E. the President directs immediate rollout of 200,000 affordable units nationwide, including Trans-Nzoia allocation.

Jan 2023
Financial

Housing Levy Activation

1.5% Housing Levy takes effect. KRA begins collection via eCitizen. Boma Yangu portal opens for beneficiary registration.

Mar 2023
County Govt

Trans-Nzoia Land Allocation MoU

Governor signs MoU with AHB — 5 parcels of county public land formally set aside for AHP sites across the five constituencies.

Jul 2023
Oversight

NEMA EIAs Completed

Environmental Impact Assessments completed for all 5 priority sites. NEMA grants conditional approval with mitigation requirements.

Sep 2023
Community

Ward Baraza Series — 5 Constituencies

Public participation barazas held in each ward. Over 1,200 residents attend. Community grievances recorded and addressed in project designs.

Dec 2023
Contractors

First 4 Contracts Awarded

NCA-registered firms awarded contracts for Kitale, Matunda, Saboti and Kiminini sites following open competitive tendering.

Feb 2024
National Govt

Trans-Nzoia Groundbreaking Ceremony

CS for Housing officiates groundbreaking at Maili Tatu, Kitale. Construction works commence at Maili Tatu and Matunda sites simultaneously.

Jun 2024
Oversight

NCA Q1 Site Audit — All Sites Pass

National Construction Authority conducts first quarterly inspection. All active sites pass structural quality checks. Minor drainage issues flagged at Kiminini — resolved.

Jan 2025
Community

Beneficiary Balloting — Phase 1

AHB conducts first transparent allocation ballot for 400 units. 1,840 applicants verified; priority given to teachers, nurses and PWD groups.

Present
Contractors

8 Sites Active — 1,730 Units Underway

All eight Trans-Nzoia AHP sites now under active construction. Maili Tatu leads at 60% completion. Programme on track for Q4 2026 first handovers.

Get Involved

How to Engage the Programme

Whether you are a potential beneficiary, a community leader, a journalist or an interested partner — here is how to connect with the Trans-Nzoia AHP.

Register as a Beneficiary

Apply for an affordable housing unit through the national Boma Yangu portal. All Trans-Nzoia County residents who contribute to the Housing Levy are eligible to apply.

  • 1 Create account on Boma Yangu
  • 2 Upload ID, KRA PIN & payslip
  • 3 Select Trans-Nzoia as preferred county
  • 4 Await allocation ballot notification
Apply on Boma Yangu

Report a Site Issue

Noticed a construction quality concern, delayed works or a governance issue? Submit a formal grievance through our Field Office or NEMA's environmental desk.

  • 1 Document the concern with date & location
  • 2 Contact the Kitale Field Office
  • 3 Or submit via our Contact page
  • 4 We respond within 5 working days
Contact Field Office

Attend a Community Baraza

Ward-level public participation meetings are held quarterly at each project site. All residents are welcome to attend, ask questions and provide input into the programme.

  • 1 Watch for announcements on this tracker
  • 2 Notify your ward administrator
  • 3 Bring your National ID
  • 4 Sign the attendance register
See upcoming events
